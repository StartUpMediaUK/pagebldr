# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback,
responsibility, and respect for differing viewpoints. Unacceptable behavior
includes harassment, sexualized attention, insults, threats, doxxing, and other
conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful.
Report conduct concerns privately to the Startup Media project owners. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.
