---
title: Audit, analytics, consent, and developer access
description: Deliver distinct schema-versioned events to Host-configured sinks.
---

Audit events describe durable authoring and lifecycle mutations. Analytics
events describe consent-aware Visits and Interactions. The Host selects sinks
and owns retention, access, querying, export, and privacy policy.

The documentation site ships without audience analytics in the initial alpha.
This avoids setting cookies or sending visitor data before a provider, purpose,
retention period, and consent policy are approved. Operational hosting logs are
controlled by the deployment owner and are not a pagebldr analytics sink.
